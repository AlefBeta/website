Alef Beta Neapolis

PARTIALLY OCCUPIED
THE PROJECT

Contemporary living in the heart of Enaerios.

Alef Beta Neapolis is a residential development in Enaerios, Limassol, comprising two buildings and 30 apartments. The first building, ten apartments, was completed and has been occupied since 2025; the second, twenty apartments, is on track for mid-2026. Ground was broken in 2024, and the development includes a shared pool and fully equipped gym in addition to its residential units.
