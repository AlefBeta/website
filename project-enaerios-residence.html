Enaerios Residence

UNDER CONSTRUCTION
THE PROJECT

Contemporary living in the heart of Enaerios.

Ground was broken in Enaerios in 2023, on a site chosen for a simple reason: it sits at the point where the neighborhood's quiet residential streets meet the pace of central Limassol. Two buildings rise from that site, conceived as a single composition — not a repeated block, but a range of homes built to different rhythms of life, from a compact one-bedroom to a three-bedroom apartment meant to be lived in for decades.

The commitment through every stage has been the same: floor plans that flex to how people actually live, and a material palette — pale stone against dark timber accents — chosen to age well rather than simply photograph well. Occupancy is expected in 2024.
