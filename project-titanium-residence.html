MESA GEITONIA, LIMASSOL

Titanium Residence

UNDER CONSTRUCTION
THE PROJECT

Hillside luxury, city views.

The site for Titanium Residence was chosen for a view most hillside plots in Mesa Geitonia don't have: city, sea, and mountains from the same balcony. Rather than build to maximize unit count, the brief called for six 2-bedroom apartments only — each one wide enough that every room, not just the living area, reaches the view.

Expansive balconies and a facade of white stone against deep grey carry that same restraint through the exterior. The location does the rest: adjacent to the Grammar School, a short drive from Foleys, and close enough to Limassol's center to make the quiet of the hillside feel like a choice rather than a compromise.
