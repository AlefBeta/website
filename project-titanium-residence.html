Titanium Residence

PRE-CONSTRUCTION
THE PROJECT

Hillside luxury, city views.

Titanium Residence is a premier hillside development offering breathtaking views of the city, sea, and mountains — a collection of six 2-bedroom apartments where sophistication and location converge. The residence's elegant design, featuring expansive balconies and a striking contrast of white stone with deep grey, reimagines stylish urban living in Limassol.

Adjacent to the Grammar School and a short drive from leading schools like Foleys, Titanium Residence positions education, leisure, and lifestyle at the doorstep, with quick highway access and proximity to bars, supermarkets, restaurants, and gyms.
