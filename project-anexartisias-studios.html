LIMASSOL OLD TOWN

Alef Beta Studios

COMPLETED
THE PROJECT

Luxury living in the heart of the old city.

Alef Beta Studios sits inside Limassol's old town, on a street where low-rise houses with terracotta roofs still stand beside newer construction — the site itself dictated the brief. 18 studio apartments were planned not as a single loud gesture, but as a building in conversation with its neighbors: pale render and vertical timber screens giving way, at roof level, to a shared terrace with open views across the old city. Completed and occupied in 2025, it now stands as one of the old town's newest residential addresses.

Gated access and dedicated parking are built in from the ground up, and the location does the rest — offices, restaurants, and the seafront are all within walking distance, with Limassol Marina a short walk further.
