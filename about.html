Who we are.

A privately owned real estate company based in Limassol, active across Cyprus, Israel and Greece.

OUR STORY

Experience across markets. One clear standard.

Alef Beta is a privately owned real estate development company based in Limassol, Cyprus. Our team brings together over 40 years of combined experience in residential and commercial real estate, with expertise spanning development, project supervision and property management.

Our activities span Cyprus, Israel and Greece, with Limassol at the centre of our current development activity.

OUR VISION

Thoughtful development. Long-term value.

We approach every project with the same priorities: a strong location, thoughtful design, quality construction and careful execution. Our aim is to create properties that work well today and retain their value over time.

OUR EXPERTISE

What we bring to every project.

  • 01

    We develop residential and commercial properties from initial concept and planning through construction and completion, working closely with architects, engineers, contractors and consultants throughout the process.

  • 02

Our involvement does not necessarily end when construction is complete. Through our property management services, we continue to support owners and investors with the ongoing operation, maintenance and financial management of their properties.
