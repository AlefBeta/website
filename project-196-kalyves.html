KALYVES, CRETE

196 Kalyves

PRE-CONSTRUCTION
THE PROJECT

Where village soul meets coastal calm.

196 Kalyves takes its name literally: a sanctuary 196 steps from the sand, on a stretch of coastline where the Aegean meets the Lefka Ori mountains. Fifty-eight residences — studio to penthouse — are built from authentic Cretan sandstone and Travertine marble, with floor-to-ceiling glazing that turns the sea into part of the interior.

The project is conceived as a wellness destination as much as a residence: dual infinity pools, a yoga studio, gym, and spa are shared across the community, and every home is delivered curated and operationally ready — down to the furniture.
