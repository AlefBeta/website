THE PROJECT

A purpose-built address for medical professionals.

Located in Agia Zoni, just 50 metres from Agia Polyclinic, this contemporary commercial building is being purposefully designed as a dedicated destination for doctors, specialists and private clinics.

With a total covered area of 1,250 m² across four floors, the building will provide flexible spaces that can be configured to meet the individual requirements of modern medical practices.

A raised-floor system will allow medical, electrical, plumbing and data infrastructure to be installed discreetly and adapted to the needs of each practice. A welcoming ground-floor lobby with reception, two elevators, dedicated parking and a rooftop terrace will complete the building.

Combining a strategic location with flexible planning and purpose-designed infrastructure, the project will offer a professional and contemporary environment created specifically for modern healthcare.
