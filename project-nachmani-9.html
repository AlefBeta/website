TEL AVIV

Nachmani 9

SOLD OUT
THE PROJECT

An architectural landmark in the heart of Tel Aviv.

Nachmani 9 occupies a corner site in the heart of Tel Aviv, and the design leans into that exposure rather than softening it: a white cube cut through with dramatic black-framed openings and cantilevered balconies, visible from both streets it fronts. Ten apartments across four floors, each with the same clarity of material — white render, black steel, glass — carried down to the last detail.

The building sold out ahead of completion, a result the design team attributes to restraint as much as ambition: nothing was added to the facade that didn't need to be there.
